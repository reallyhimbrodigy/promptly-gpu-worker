**Timeline** (MEDIUM) — a vertical rail of numbered station-nodes with a label card beside each; an accent fill travels node→node and a comet head rides it, each station popping active as the fill docks. Claim: "Here's the PROCESS, step by step." **FITS:** MOMENT SHAPE — use when the dialogue walks ordered stages of ONE journey, named but UNDATED ("first we research, then design, then build, then ship") — 2–5 named steps, shown together on one rail. educational, explainer, business, how-to. **FIGHTS:** the same stages carrying TIME labels, or a scenic winding-path version → TimelineRoadmap; the script announcing its running position → StepDivider; a single quantity advancing toward a target → ProgressBar; unordered parallel items → StickyNotes or RankedList. viral-fast, cinematic — structural and deliberate.
