**BarRace** (MEDIUM) — a centered stack of 2–4 horizontal bars growing to lengths proportional to their values; each has a caps label and a value counting up at the bar's tip, and the highest flips to gold with a crown as it pulls ahead. Claim: "Watch these values race — the biggest wins." **FITS:** MOMENT SHAPE — use when the dialogue compares two-plus countable quantities head-to-head and the COMPARISON is the point ("we did 40K, they did 28K, the rest barely 12K"). business, educational, competitive, data-viral. **FIGHTS:** one bar toward a goal → ProgressBar; a single headline number → StatCard. cinematic, story, calm — a racing chart is analytical, wrong for an emotional beat.
