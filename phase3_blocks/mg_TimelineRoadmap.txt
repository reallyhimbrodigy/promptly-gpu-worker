**TimelineRoadmap** (MEDIUM) — a winding S-curve "road" of numbered stations with a comet that travels the path, each station popping active with its label + time sublabel as the comet docks (the scenic-route variant of Timeline). Claim: "Here's the journey, stage by stage." **FITS:** MOMENT SHAPE — use for a phased roadmap whose stages carry TIME sublabels, where a flowing path suits the story better than a straight rail — plans with time sublabels ("Discovery — Week 1 → Launch — Week 6"), 3–5 stations. business, educational, planning/startup. **FIGHTS:** the same ordered stages with NO dates → Timeline (a straight node-to-node process rail); a single advancing quantity → ProgressBar. viral-fast, cinematic, casual — scenic but structural.
