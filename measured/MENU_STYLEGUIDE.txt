A NOTE ON THE LOUDNESS COLUMN, because it will mislead you the same way it misled us. browse_assets reports INTEGRATED LUFS, whose measurement window is about 400ms. Four of these sounds are shorter than that — camera-flash 235ms, popsfx 217ms, swoosh 254ms, mouse-click 151ms — so all four report -70.0 LUFS while peaking between -5.4 dB and -0.7 dB. They are not quiet. A sound shorter than the window has no valid integrated reading at all, and -70.0 there means UNMEASURABLE, not silent.
